[Evidences]
1.	The Murder Knife
Arif's blood was found on the blade. The handle had been carefully wiped. The knife wound was severe, but later toxicology established that it was not the cause of death.
2.	The Poison
An unidentified poison was found in Arif's bloodstream. It appears to have been administered shortly before his death.
3.	The Puncture Mark
A tiny puncture was discovered on Arif's upper arm. Initially dismissed as irrelevant, it may correspond to the administration of the poison.
4.	The Missing Notebook
Arif's steel vault was opened without visible damage. The notebook containing his investigation was never recovered.
5.	The Burned Page
A partially burned sheet was found inside the fireplace. Only the words "EVIL EYE'S WRATH" remained readable.
6.	The Telephone Record
Arindom claimed his final conversation with Arif lasted less than two minutes. The telephone record shows six minutes.
7.	The Bloodstained Cuff
A small amount of Arif's blood was found on Durjo's sleeve. His explanation that he tried to help Arif cannot be independently confirmed.
8.	The Photograph Archive
Several photographs connected to Arif's investigation disappeared from the newspaper archive shortly before his death.
9.	Sultana's Statement
Sultana identified Jawer as one of the men present. She never explicitly stated that Jawer killed Arif.
