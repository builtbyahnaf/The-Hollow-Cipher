[Interrogation]
[Jawer Chakrovarty]:
Jawer initially denies entering the house. When confronted with evidence placing him near Green Road, he changes his story. He admits entering the house but insists he came after receiving a message from Arif. He claims the study was already in chaos when he arrived. He remembers seeing Durjo. He does not remember seeing Arindom.
When asked about the notebook, Jawer says:
"I knew Arif was working on something. I didn't know what."
When asked why Sultana identified him, he becomes silent.
Then says:
"Because she saw me."
He refuses to explain what he means.
[Arindom Kumar Biswas]:
Arindom describes Arif as an increasingly reckless journalist who had become obsessed with an investigation he could not prove. He insists he tried to protect Arif from publishing "unverified nonsense." He denies knowing about the notebook. He also claims his final conversation with Arif lasted less than two minutes.
Telephone records show it lasted six.
When confronted, Arindom says Arif had forgotten to hang up. He smiles when saying it.
[Durjo Das]:
Durjo admits he knew about the investigation. He claims Arif had accused someone inside the newspaper of destroying evidence. Durjo refuses to say who. He admits being inside the house that night. He says he arrived after hearing Arif scream. His shirt contained a small trace of Arif's blood. He claims he tried to help him. When asked whether he saw Jawer holding the knife, he answers:
"I saw Jawer holding something."
When pressed, he changes it to:
"I think it was the knife."
[ Mahir Hossain ]:
Mahir photographed several of the dead animals connected to Arif's investigation. The photographs disappeared from the newspaper archive. He claims he does not know where they went. He says Arif had recently become frightened of someone at work. He refuses to name the person.
[Nayeem Ahmed]:
Nayeem managed the newspaper's physical archives. He admits that Arif frequently requested old reports concerning unexplained animal deaths. He denies ever seeing the notebook.
One recovered page contains his handwriting. He claims Arif must have copied something from the archive himself.
[Shafiq Rahman]:
Shafiq was one of Arif's confidential sources. He admits warning Arif to abandon the investigation. He claims the reason was simple:
"Some stories aren't worth dying for."
He denies knowing about Evil Eye's Wrath. When shown the phrase, however, he immediately asks:
"Where did you find that?"
[Kamal Uddin]:
Kamal was responsible for the residential block's night security. He admits that someone asked him about the electrical panel that evening. He initially says he cannot remember who.
Later, he remembers that the person showed him what appeared to be an official identification card. He refuses to describe it.
