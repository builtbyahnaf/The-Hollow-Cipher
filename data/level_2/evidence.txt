1. The Blood-Stained Letter
Found in the victim's pocket. Partially burned. Written in hurried handwriting. Mentions a meeting at the lighthouse on the night of the disappearance. The ink matches the typewriter ribbon from the suspect's office.

2. The Lighthouse Key
A brass key with "East Point Light" stamped on the tag. Found in the desk drawer of Suspect 3. Fingerprints match both the victim and Suspect 3.

3. The Torn Photograph
Recovered from the fireplace. Shows the victim with Suspect 2 at a private dinner two weeks before the incident. The back reads "Insurance policy - just in case."

4. The Missing Ledger Page
The victim's financial ledger has a page torn out. The adjacent page shows a payment of $50,000 to "J. R." dated three days before the disappearance.

5. The Anonymous Tip
A letter received by police two days after the incident. Claims the victim was planning to expose a smuggling ring operating through the harbor. Handwriting analysis matches Suspect 1.
