[Past History]
[ Jonathan Reeves ]
Former customs officer. Dismissed after an internal investigation into missing cargo manifests. Known to have connections with harbor smuggling rings. The victim was the prosecutor who built the case against him.

[ Margaret Holloway ]
Heiress to the Holloway Shipping fortune. Her family's company was under investigation by the victim for tax evasion and illegal dumping. The victim had evidence that could seize their assets.

[ Captain Elias Vane ]
Lighthouse keeper for 30 years. The lighthouse was decommissioned two years ago but he refused to leave. The victim was pushing for the property to be sold to developers. Vane has a sealed juvenile record for arson.

[ Dr. Adrian Croft ]
Respected physician with a secret practice prescribing controlled substances to wealthy clients. The victim discovered his side business during the smuggling investigation. The victim was his most leverageable patient.

[ Officer Raymond Pike ]
20-year veteran. Passed over for promotion three times. The victim was the key witness in an internal affairs investigation against Pike's former partner. Pike blames the victim for his stalled career.

[ The Missing Ledger ]
The victim maintained a detailed ledger of all bribes and payoffs in the harbor operation. One page - the most recent - is missing. It reportedly contained names of all active participants and payment amounts.
