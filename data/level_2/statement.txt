[Time] 14th March, 1997 | 11:47 PM
[Location] East Point Lighthouse, Harbor District
[Statement]
The fog was thicker than usual that night. Officer Pike arrived first, flashlight cutting through the mist. He found the victim's body at the base of the lighthouse stairs. The official report said accidental fall. But the bruising on the wrists suggested restraint. The sedative in the bloodstream matched Dr. Croft's prescription. The lighthouse key in the victim's pocket had been used that night - the lock showed fresh wear.

The torn photograph in the fireplace. The anonymous tip in Pike's desk. The missing ledger page. Every piece pointed to a conspiracy, not an accident.

Pike closed the case in 48 hours. "Accidental death. No foul play suspected." His partner's name was on the missing ledger page. So was the Chief of Police. So was the Mayor's nephew.

The victim wasn't just investigating smuggling. He was building a RICO case against the entire harbor power structure. And someone made sure he never filed it.

The lighthouse still stands. Vane still tends it, though the light hasn't worked in years. Sometimes, on foggy nights, locals swear they see a figure at the top window. Writing. Watching. Waiting for someone to read the page that was never found.

[Question]
Who orchestrated the victim's death and why?
