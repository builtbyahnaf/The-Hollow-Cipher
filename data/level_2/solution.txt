Jawer Chakrovarty
[Explanation]
Jawer Chakrovarty stole Arif Rahman's notebook during the original murder and concealed it to prevent the investigation into Evil Eye's Wrath from exposing his involvement.

His archive requests and attempts to obtain duplicate photographs were part of the same effort to control or remove the records connected to Arif's findings. His contradictory statements about the photographs and his undisclosed knowledge of the vault establish that he deliberately concealed his actions.

Durjo Das appeared to be the likely culprit because of his suspicious conduct and his involvement in the newspaper's records. However, no evidence establishes that he stole the notebook. Jawer's actions, by contrast, directly connect him to the materials that disappeared.

Arindom Kumar Biswas was subsequently killed to prevent the investigation from uncovering what he knew. The circumstances of his death point toward the same conspiracy, although the precise method remains unexplained.
Jawer is therefore the culprit responsible for stealing the notebook. His connection to a subgroup of Evil Eye's Wrath explains his motive for suppressing Arif's investigation, while the remaining evidence establishes the broader conspiracy behind the two deaths.
