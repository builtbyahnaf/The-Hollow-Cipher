[Time] 1st December, 1887 | 11:30 AM
[Location] The Palassy Ground, Dhaka
[Statement]
The wall beneath the eastern wing did not break like ordinary masonry. When the investigators finally opened it, the bricks came away from the inside, one by one, as though someone had been patiently removing them for years. Behind them lay a narrow passage that descended beneath the newspaper archive. The air smelled of wet earth, burnt chemicals, and something faintly sweet that had no business surviving underground.
The chamber at its end was far larger than the building above could have permitted. Its walls were covered with maps of Bengal, each marked with black pins, red threads, and small photographs of people who had never met one another. Dhaka, Chittagong, Rajshahi, Sylhet, Mymensingh, and towns beyond the reach of the newspaper's circulation had all been marked. Beside each location were names, dates, and descriptions of incidents that had not yet occurred. A village fire. A child who would speak in an unfamiliar voice. A magistrate found kneeling before a mirror. A family that would disappear from a locked house, leaving every door secured from within.
At first, the investigator assumed the documents described predictions. Then the first report was found beneath a photograph of a house that had not yet burned. The report described the fire in the past tense. It named the dead, described the position of their bodies, and recorded the testimony of a witness who would not be born for another six years. Every page bore the same mark: an eye enclosed within a rhombus, crossed through as though someone had tried to destroy it and failed.
The documents revealed the actual ambition of the Evil Eye's Wrath. It was no isolated circle of frightened men whispering over forbidden books. It was an organisation preparing to spread across the nation, using staged miracles, disappearances, blackmail, and carefully arranged deaths to make the population believe that an invisible power had begun judging the living. Its followers would create the terror, then offer protection from it. Its enemies would become examples. Its believers would become informants. Its victims would become martyrs. In every district, fear would be given a different face, but every face would look toward the same Eye.
Then the investigator found the photographs.
Arif Rahman stood beside a window in his study, alive and apparently unaware that he was being photographed. Arindom Kumar Biswas sat at his desk with his hands folded, his eyes open. Jawer Chakrovarty appeared in a room whose walls were covered with the cult's symbol. The three photographs had been taken on different dates, in different places, under different lighting. Yet each man had a dark circle beneath his feet, like the shadow cast by an object that was not present.
The fourth photograph showed the investigator entering the chamber.
It had been taken from behind.
There was no second entrance. No window. No opening through which a photographer could have watched the passage. The plate was still damp, and the image continued to darken while the investigator held it. In the photograph, the investigator was carrying the notebook recovered from Jawer. In reality, the notebook remained locked in the archive above.
The investigator turned the plate over.
Written on its reverse was a sentence in the same handwriting as the anonymous message:
YOU WERE NOT FOLLOWING THE EVIDENCE. THE EVIDENCE WAS FOLLOWING YOU.
A sound came from the far end of the chamber. Three slow knocks.
The investigator raised the lantern.
At the end of the room stood a tall photographic screen, its black cloth hanging motionless in the still air. A human silhouette was visible behind it. The figure did not move when addressed. It did not move when approached. Only when the investigator stepped to one side did the silhouette appear to turn its head.
There was no one behind the screen.
On the table beside it lay a freshly developed photograph of the investigator standing exactly where they stood now, holding the lantern in the same hand, looking toward the same empty screen.
The photograph had captured the next moment as well.
In it, the investigator's mouth was open, screaming.

[Question]
Who orchestrated the nationwide conspiracy?
