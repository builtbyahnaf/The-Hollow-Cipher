Mahir Hossain orchestrated the nationwide conspiracy behind the Evil Eye's Wrath. The national ledger documents a coordinated network of local operations. The photographic plates place Mahir at the centre of the events surrounding Arif, Arindom, and Jawer. The anonymous message was produced using materials from his studio and was deliberately constructed to guide the investigator toward selected discoveries. Mahir's own statements confirm that he shaped the investigation and manipulated the fears and ambitions of the cult's members.
The curses were not separate mysteries. They were consequences of the same chain of events. Arif was silenced when his investigation threatened to expose the conspiracy. Arindom's attempt to conceal his complicity did not free him from its consequences. Jawer's theft bound him to the secret he hoped to possess. Each man believed he could control one part of the situation. Each discovered that his choices had already become part of something larger.
Mahir's method depended on more than lying. He used true evidence to guide false conclusions, staged events to reinforce genuine fears, and made the investigator believe that every discovery was an independent victory. The anonymous message was not a warning. It was the first step in a carefully arranged investigation.
Yet the final evidence prevents the case from closing cleanly. The impossible photographs, the future dated records, and the investigator's unexplained handwriting cannot be dismissed as ordinary tricks. Mahir may have exploited these phenomena, but the evidence does not prove that he created them. His confession establishes his responsibility for the conspiracy, not his mastery over everything that occurred within it.
As the investigator prepares to leave the archive, a final photographic plate appears beneath the locked door. It shows the national ledger burning in the chamber. Every page has turned to ash except the one bearing the investigator's name.
On the reverse is a final message:
THE EYE HAS BEEN FOUND. THE EYE HAS BEEN NAMED. THE EYE HAS BEEN GIVEN A FACE.
Beneath it, in handwriting the investigator recognises as their own, is one last sentence:
NOW IT CAN LOOK THROUGH YOU.
